OK Jaanu

The Story of Gopi & Charu

While all the other legends show the fiery passion of young love... Gopi Uncle and Charu Auntie showed us something rarer and even more beautiful. They showed us the magic of growing old together. Even as Alzheimer's made Charu forget her own life, Gopi never left her side. He became her memory. He remembered their beautiful story for the both of them.

"That is the exact love I want with you, Jaanu. I don't just want the exciting beginnings. I want the quiet moments, the gray hairs, and the wrinkled smiles. If you ever forget, I promise to hold your hand and remind you of our beautiful story every single day, until the very end."
